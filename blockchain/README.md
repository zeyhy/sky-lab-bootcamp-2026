## 📚 What I Learned

Blockchain can be understood as:

> **Block + Chain = Blockchain**

Each new piece of information is added to a block and connected to the previous block, forming a continuous chain.

One of the key characteristics of blockchain is that once information is recorded, it cannot be easily changed or altered.

## ⚙️ How Does Blockchain Work?

The basic process can be summarized in four steps:

1. A transaction takes place.
2. The network verifies the transaction.
3. A new block is created.
4. The block is added to the chain.

## ✅ Advantages

- Data is difficult to alter once recorded.
- Transactions can be verified by the network.
- The chain provides a traceable record of information.
- It can reduce the need for a central authority in some applications.

## ⚠️ Disadvantages

- Some blockchain systems can require high amounts of computational resources.
- Transaction processing can be slower than some centralized systems.
- Blockchain technology can be complex to understand and implement.
- Different blockchain systems have different limitations and use cases.

## 💡 Key Takeaway

The most important concept I learned is how individual blocks are connected to each other to create a chain of information.

This helped me understand the basic logic behind blockchain technology and why recorded information is designed to be difficult to change.
